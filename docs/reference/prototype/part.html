Release notes

+Added

  • +Generates a valid four-year plan for any CS stream in under 300 ms.
  • +Co-op terms as first-class blocks, so work terms cannot be double-booked.
  • +Property-based tests over 10,000 random plans in CI.

~Changed

  • ~Replaced brute-force search with constraint propagation once plans took 4 s.
  • ~Moved plan state into the URL so advisors can open a student's plan from a link.

!Known issues

  • !Offerings are scraped, so a timetable change breaks plans until the next sync.
  • !If I rebuilt it I would ask the registrar for a data feed before writing a scraper.

General notes

  1. 1.
    Constraint propagation over searchChose arc consistency to prune impossible terms first, instead of backtracking over every ordering, because the search space grew with every elective and 4 s felt broken.
  2. 2.
    Postgres over a document storeChose relational tables for courses and prerequisites, instead of nesting them in documents, because prerequisites are a graph that needed querying in both directions.
